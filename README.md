DAYFLOW is a personal productivity and daily routine management Android application designed to help you organize your day, build better habits, manage tasks, and stay consistent with your goals.

Instead of juggling multiple productivity applications, DAYFLOW brings your daily planning and essential routines together in one place.

## Features

### Daily Planning
- Organize your day with a structured daily roadmap.
- Plan activities and manage priorities.
- Track daily progress and completion.

### Habit Tracking
- Create everyday habits.
- Track consistency through daily logs.
- Monitor your progress over time.

### Task Management
- Organize tasks by priority.
- Break larger tasks into smaller subtasks.
- Keep track of pending and completed work.

### Ideas & Notes
- Capture ideas whenever inspiration strikes.
- Keep important thoughts and notes organized.

### Alarms & Timers
- Create alarms and reminders.
- Use timers to manage focused activities.
- Receive notifications for scheduled activities.

### Scheduled WhatsApp Messages
- Schedule messages for birthdays, anniversaries, festivals, and other occasions.
- Prepare and edit messages in advance.
- Open WhatsApp with your message ready to send.

**Note:** DAYFLOW does not automatically send WhatsApp messages. You remain in control and manually press Send.

### Insights & Daily Reviews
- Review your daily performance.
- Track completed activities.
- Understand your consistency and progress.

### Data Backup & Restore
- Export your application data into JSON.
- Restore your information whenever needed.
- Keep your personal productivity data under your control.

## Download & Installation

**Latest Release: v0.6.0**

1. Visit the [Latest Release](https://github.com/RizarHahs/DayFlow/releases/latest) page.
2. Download `app-release.apk`.
3. Open the downloaded APK on your Android device.
4. Allow installation from unknown sources if prompted.
5. Install and launch DAYFLOW.

**Compatibility:** Android 8.0 (API 26) and above.

---

## Privacy & Data

- No account registration is required.
- Your productivity data is stored locally on your device.
- JSON backup and restore are available.
- Scheduled WhatsApp messages require WhatsApp to complete the sending process manually.

---

## Technology Stack

- **Language:** Kotlin
- **UI:** Jetpack Compose
- **Architecture:** MVVM
- **Database:** Room
- **Dependency Injection:** Hilt
- **Background Tasks:** AlarmManager, BroadcastReceivers
- **Build System:** Gradle
